# 10 Graph algorithms and traversal

Module: ALG
Root: ALG-0
Scope: The graph algorithms and walks of the engine (iris_vector_graph/_engine/algorithms.py) and the IRIS classes that
answer them: Graph.KG.Algorithms (WCC, CDLP), PageRank, PageRankEmbedded, Centrality, Communities, Subgraph, Traversal,
TraversalBFS, TraversalKHop, TraversalPaths, VarLength; the interned snapshot ^NKG and its readers NKGAccel,
NKGAccelAdjacency, NKGAccelCentrality, NKGAccelLoader, NKGAccelTraversal; the Arno library bridge ArnoAccel and
iris_vector_graph/stores/arno_bridge.py; Native Reader routing and the Proof Record (iris_vector_graph/arno_readers.py,
iris_vector_graph/_arno_gate.py); and the shared result cache (Graph.KG.AlgoCache, iris_vector_graph/algo_cache.py) as
the store (iris_vector_graph/stores/iris_sql_store.py) uses it. The adjacency index ^KG and its writers belong to the
adjacency module; this module reads them. Cypher procedure parsing belongs to the Cypher module; only the refusals of the
^NKG procedures are stated here.
