# 02 Graph identity

Module: GID
Root: GID-0
Scope: Graph names and their validation, the two graph-key derivations, graph modes (multigraph) and edge identity, and the all-graphs read and write rule. Production files: iris_src/src/Graph/KG/GraphKey.cls, iris_src/src/Graph/KG/GraphMode.cls, iris_vector_graph/_validate.py (graph-name and input validation), the graph-scoping parts of iris_vector_graph/engine.py, and the mode commands set_multigraph / is_multigraph in iris_vector_graph/_engine/admin.py. The ledger, Bolt, verify and Cypher surfaces are cited only where they decide graph scope or edge identity; their own behaviour belongs to their modules.
