# 05 Temporal edges and the temporal index

Module: TMP
Root: TMP-0
Scope: Temporal edges and the temporal index of every graph: writes (insert, update, skip, collisions), bucket aggregates and analytics, the one-string window reads and the paged window reader, the latest-event index, purges and the erase hooks of the temporal stores, temporal-only predicates and the marked structural row a temporal edge implies, the upgrade backfills, interned label sets, named time zones, and how temporal writes relate to the ledger. Production files: iris_src/src/Graph/KG/TemporalIndex.cls, iris_src/src/Graph/KG/TemporalIndexMS.cls, iris_src/src/Graph/KG/TemporalPolicy.cls, iris_src/src/Graph/KG/TemporalWindow.cls, iris_vector_graph/_engine/temporal.py, iris_vector_graph/_engine/temporal_window.py, iris_vector_graph/_engine/temporal_only_warning.py, iris_vector_graph/_tz.py, iris_vector_graph/migrations/temporal_structural_rows.py. The marked-row writers in Graph.KG.Adjacency (AssertTemporalRow, RetractTemporalRow(s)) and Graph.KG.LedgerApply.OpCreateTemporalEdge are cited where this module's commands call them; their other behaviour belongs to the adjacency and ledger modules.
