# 13 Ledger redaction

Module: RED
Root: RED-0
Scope: Graph.KG.LedgerRedact (iris_src/src/Graph/KG/LedgerRedact.cls), the redaction checks in Graph.KG.Ledger.CommitInTxn
(Ledger.cls:466-473, 514-518), the one exemption in Graph.KG.LedgerRevision.RejectUpdate (LedgerRevision.cls:77-101),
GraphLedger.redact and redaction_in_progress (iris_vector_graph/ledger/client.py:631-693) and the redaction errors
(iris_vector_graph/ledger/errors.py:129-170). Worked example of the uplift: modelled in proofs/src (L1, L2), model-checked in
model/src, observed on IRIS Community in native/scenarios.py. Where a statement is proved, the proof is about the L2 machine of
the reviewed design (proofs/src/l2.rs). The code at the pin corresponds to the controls `control_unmarked_start` and
`control_other_target` together, which the proof rejects; L2 does not model audit counts, statement entries, unattributed
records or a final step without a marker (independent review, REVIEW.md). Notes say which.
