Module: ERS
Root: ERS-0
Scope: Graph.KG.Eraser (iris_src/src/Graph/KG/Eraser.cls), the store inventory Graph.KG.GraphStores
(iris_src/src/Graph/KG/GraphStores.cls), and the engine entry points erase_graph and delete_nodes_by_prefix
