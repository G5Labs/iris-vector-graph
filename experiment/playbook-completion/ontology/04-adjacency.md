# 04 Adjacency index, degree cache and rebuilt stores

Module: ADJ
Root: ADJ-0
Scope: The native adjacency of every graph and what is derived from it. Production files:
iris_src/src/Graph/KG/Adjacency.cls (the one writer), AdjIndex.cls (the row hook on rdf_edges), Adj.cls (degree reads
and the degree cache), EdgeScan.cls (scans and the old write names, now delegations), Edgeless.cls (nodes with no
edge), GraphIndex.cls (interning helpers of the interned snapshot), TraversalBuild.cls (BuildKG, BuildNKG, two-hop
counts), GraphVerify.cls (verify_graph), AlgoCache.cls (the result cache); iris_vector_graph/_edgeless.py,
freshness.py, algo_cache.py; and the engine's sync, rebuild, verify and status entry points
(iris_vector_graph/_engine/schema.py sync, rebuild_kg, rebuild_nkg, backfill_degp; _engine/admin.py verify_sync,
verify_graph, status().adjacency; _engine/nodes_edges.py bulk_delete_adjacency and the bulk-load warm-up;
stores/iris_sql_store.py _cached_algorithm). Erasure (Eraser.cls), temporal storage (TemporalIndex.cls), the ledger and
snapshot archives are other modules; this one states only how they reach the adjacency.
