## Limits

- The contract's clauses hold inside its semantic subset (CONTRACT.md 'Outside the semantic subset'); everything outside is a family above, assumed unless a run says otherwise.
- A family's 'observed' runs are lane runs in which its consequence occurred (often a product finding); they do not validate the premise.
- Proof premises keep the verdict of `premise_tests.json`; this file adds no judgement to them.
