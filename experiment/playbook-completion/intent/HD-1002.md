# HD-1002 Should label propagation say when its labels did not settle?

## Contradictions

- `docs/COMMUNITIES.md:205-208 (CDLP silent, af2bc84e 2026-11-05)` vs `algorithms.py:1445-1446 (eigenvector raises, 99b00ce9 2026-11-07); algorithms.py:808 (WCC raises)`: Not a contradiction of one contract. It is inconsistent policy across iterative algorithms: CDLP is documented as silent, while WCC and eigenvector refuse an unconverged answer.

## As built

On the path a->b->c, kg_CDLP(max_iterations=k) for k=1..6 alternates between two labelings with period 2. The answer is a plain {node: label} dict with no convergence key, warning or flag.

Evidence: live `ib-HD1002-cdlp-oscillation`; inspected `iris_vector_graph/_engine/algorithms.py:829-848`

## Intended

Status: **supported**. The current public contract is option (c): a fixed-iteration synchronous CDLP that returns the last round with no in-band signal. The documented diagnostic is to compare k with k+1. The maintainer documented this deliberately (af2bc84e W6). A settledness flag would be a compatible strengthening, not part of the contract.

## Working assumption

Keep the return shape. Treat the documented k vs k+1 comparison as the convergence diagnostic. Models and oracles must not assume CDLP converges. A flag such as a _meta entry or warning is optional.

Changed from decisions.md: **yes**. decisions.md treated 'expose whether labels settled' as the working assumption. The maintainer's W6 documentation (af2bc84e) settles the current contract as documented-silent, so the status moves from assumption to supported for (c). The flag becomes an enhancement.

## Alternatives

- (a) raise like WCC/eigenvector (changes consumers of best-effort labels)
- (b) answer and flag non-convergence (_meta row or warning)
- (c) silent and documented [current, supported]
