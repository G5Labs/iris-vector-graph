## Contradictions

- Callers are told [] means 'no index'; an existing index mid-build answers partial or empty without error, and IVFIndex.Info reported indexed=0 while 12 vectors answered after a killed rebuild. (`iris_vector_graph/_engine/vector.py:2029-2031`; `live intent-a-hd0602-ivf-partial`)

## As built

Live: during a real ivf_build rebuild, 35 of 44 concurrent searches answered fewer than the 300 indexed vectors (min 7), with no error; a rebuild killed mid-way left an index answering 12 of 300 and Info showing indexed=0. VecIndex.Build and PLAIDSearch.Build have the same kill-and-refill shape (inspected, not run).

Evidence: live `intent-a-hd0602-ivf-partial`; inspected `iris_src/src/Graph/KG/VecIndex.cls:157-163; PLAIDSearch.cls:4-6`

## Intended contract

No spec or ADR states a reader or interruption contract for these builds. Maintainer precedents for derived indexes (^NKG in-place rewrite; 'destroyed adjacency' comment) favour readers seeing a complete index or a clear unavailable result. Status: assumption.

## Working assumption

Unchanged: readers see a complete published index or a clear unavailable result, including after an interrupted rebuild; the existing partial-build behaviour is the modelled baseline.

## Alternatives

- (a) build under a temporary name and swap it in at the end
- (b) per-index lock plus a 'building' marker searches refuse
- (c) keep today's behaviour and document an offline (quiesced) rebuild
- (d) a generation flag readers check before and after

## Discriminating check

probe (run): `intent-a-hd0602-ivf-partial`. fail vs the working contract (not a stated product contract): partial answers during rebuild; a killed rebuild left 12 of 300 vectors and Info indexed=0.

## What would change this

A maintainer statement that index builds are offline operations (then (c) documented), or a contract like ^NKG's.

## Residual question for maintainer

May IVF/VecIndex/PLAID searches observe a partially built index, and may an interrupted build leave one with no marker? If yes, should the docs require a quiesced (offline) rebuild?

## Consequence rank hint

medium: wrong or partial search results without error during and after an interrupted build; recoverable by rebuilding.
