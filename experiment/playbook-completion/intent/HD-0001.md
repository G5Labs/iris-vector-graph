# HD-0001: How to read "defined by its ancestors or an earlier same-level statement"

Status: **supported** (literal reading) · Confidence: high · Changed from decisions.md: no

## Question

Playbook §3.1 "Definitions first": may a statement use a term that only an earlier sibling of one of its ancestors
defines (lexical scope, the uplift's default), or only terms defined by its parent chain or by an earlier statement
with the same parent (literal reading)?

## Contradictions

- The original submission's checker (`ontology_index.py:438-450`) and its passed §3.1 gate versus the playbook text
  (lines 216-218): 1392 pairs the original admitted are out of scope under the text.
- Inside the playbook, the gate's short form "an earlier same-level statement" (line 228) taken alone would also admit a
  third reading (any earlier statement at the same depth, under any parent). The rule it summarizes (line 218) says
  "under the same parent", which excludes both that and the lexical reading.

## As built

The original uplift applied lexical scope (inspected: `ontology_index.py:438-450`); under it the gate passed. Under the
literal rule the original modules have 1392 out-of-scope statement/term pairs in 1002 statements (check, this lane;
the ontology lane's strengthened checker counts 1406 violations including 11 plural forms and 3 other kinds). The
completion's repaired copy passes the literal rule with 0 violations (ontology lane, bdcea01f).

## Intended contract

Literal: a statement's terms must be defined by the statement itself (a D statement), its parent chain, or an earlier
statement with the same parent. Status **supported**: the playbook is the authoritative method document and this
section is marked "human decision 2026-11-12"; its detailed wording settles the reading.

## Working assumption

Same as decisions.md: apply the literal rule; rewrite or reparent statements (or restate definitions locally) rather
than widen the scope; keep the strict regression check.

## Alternatives

- Lexical scope (ancestors and earlier siblings of any ancestor): would be a published change to the method.
- Same-depth reading of the gate's short form: contradicted by "under the same parent".

## Discriminating check

Check (run): `intent-a-hd0001-literal-scope`, result fail for the original submission under the literal rule (1392
pairs), consistent with `ontology-baseline-original`. No IRIS involvement.

## What would change this

A later playbook revision or human statement that widens §3.1 to lexical scope.
