# HD-1104 Should the ledger's settings be changeable after the genesis?

Question ([decisions/11.md](../../playbook-uplift/decisions/11.md)): `max_ops` and `recon_bound` are written only by
the genesis; `enable()` on an enabled ledger ignores every argument; re-enable after disable ignores `max_ops` and
`recon_bound` but overwrites strict with the value passed (default off), so `set_strict(True)`, `disable()`, `enable()`
leaves strict off and a `set_strict(True)` made while disabled is lost. Options: (a) setters, or `enable()` updates the
settings it is given and keeps strict unless passed; (b) settings fixed at genesis, documented, strict reset included;
(c) keep undocumented.

## Contradictions

None found. The contract is silent about settings after genesis; the FR texts it cites are unpublished.

## As built

Live, `ib-HD1104-settings` (pass, predictions confirmed): `enable(strict=True, max_ops=7, recon_bound=11)` on an enabled
ledger changed nothing; `disable(); enable()` reset strict to False; `set_strict(True)` on a disabled ledger was
accepted and lost on the next `enable()`; re-enable kept `max_ops=50000`, `recon_bound=250000` and took strict from
its argument.

## Intended

No authoritative source settles mutability. The contract's idempotent `enable()` and the genesis-only writes support
"bounds fixed at genesis"; nothing supports or rejects the strict reset. Status: **assumption**.

## Working assumption

Keep `max_ops` and `recon_bound` fixed at genesis. Model strict as set by `enable(strict=...)` on genesis and on every
re-enable (default False) and by `set_strict`; record the reset on re-enable as an explicit transition and do not assume
strict survives a disable/enable cycle. Changed from decisions.md: **no**.

## Alternatives

- (a) Setters, or `enable()` updating the settings passed and preserving strict when not passed (needs
  `strict: Optional[bool] = None`, a signature change, because `False` and "not passed" are indistinguishable today).
- (b) Fixed at genesis with the strict reset documented.

## Residual question for the maintainer

Is strict mode meant to survive `disable()`/`enable()`, and may `max_ops`/`recon_bound` change after genesis? Working
answer: bounds fixed; strict reset recorded as is. Best alternative: preserve strict unless passed, add setters.
