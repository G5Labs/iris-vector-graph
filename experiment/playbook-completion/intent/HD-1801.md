# HD-1801 What should a restore do to the live ledger?

Question ([decisions/18.md](../../playbook-uplift/decisions/18.md), finding F-1801): a snapshot carries all of
`^IVG.Ledger` and the ledger classes' storage; a replacing restore erases content but keeps the ledger's records
(`erase_all` kills only `stmt`/`tuple`), then writes the archive's ledger node by node on top; a merge restore does the
same without the erase. Options: (a) replace the ledger wholesale; (b) refuse when the live head is not the archive's
head unless forced; (c) never touch the ledger; (d) (b) plus re-applying live redactions after a replace.

## Contradictions

- FR-043's round trip is tested only into a wiped ledger (`PurgeAll` first); the shipped restore paths never wipe it,
  so the tested property and the shipped behaviour differ.
- The append-only, canonical ledger (CONTEXT.md:193-196, ADMIN_GUIDE.md:317) versus the overlay, which moves the head
  back below surviving revisions (live, below).

## As built

Live, `ib-HD1801-merge-restore` (fail): head 2 saved, commits 3 and 4, merge restore of the archive: `head_seq` went
back to 2 while revision rows and records 3 and 4 survived; `history()` listed seq 1-4; the next commit was refused
`LedgerInconsistencyError` ("ID key not unique ... LedgerRevisionD(3)"), so every later commit fails until manual repair;
`verify()` diverged. This refines F-1801: at the pin the overlay wedges the ledger (the `Seq` IdKey refuses reuse)
rather than interleaving records. The replacing path was not driven (shared namespace); it keeps the same ledger
subscripts (`Eraser.cls:806-812`), so the same state is predicted. The pre-redaction case is left to the orchestrator's
redaction model.

## Intended

FR-043 says only that ledger storage survives a round trip; no source says what a restore does to a different live
ledger, or to redactions made after the archive. Status: **assumption**.

## Working assumption

Treat the archive's history as a unit. When the live ledger is enabled or disabled and its head is not the archive's,
refuse before mutating anything unless the caller explicitly asks for a replacement; a replacement replaces
`^IVG.Ledger` and the ledger storage wholesale and must not bring back content that a live redaction removed. Changed
from decisions.md: **no** (the live wedge strengthens refusal before mutation).

## Alternatives

- (a) Wholesale replacement, documented (consistent with FR-043's test; can undo later redactions).
- (c) Never touch the ledger; `verify()` reports the difference.
- (d) (b) plus re-applying live redactions after a replacement.

## Discriminating check

Probe `intent/probes/lane-b/hd1801_restore_over_moved_ledger.py`, run, registry `ib-HD1801-merge-restore`, result
**fail**. Proposed minimal model for the rest: state {live revisions, head, redaction marks, archive (prefix or
divergent history)}; actions Commit, Redact, Save, Restore(mode); properties head = max revision seq,
one history per seq, no redacted content returns. Overlay violates the first; (a) violates the third when the archive
predates a redaction; (b) and (d) satisfy all three.

## Confidence and what would change it

As built: high (live, merge path). Intent: medium. A maintainer statement on restore semantics or retention would
settle it.

## Residual question for the maintainer

What should a restore do when the live ledger's head is not the archive's, including redactions made after the archive?
Working answer: refuse before mutation unless replacement is explicitly requested, and keep live redactions. Best
alternative: wholesale replacement, documented.
