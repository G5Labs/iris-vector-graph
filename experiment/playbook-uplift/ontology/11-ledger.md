# 11 Ledger core

Module: LED
Root: LED-0
Scope: The revision ledger's core at pin 3eaa79cf: enable (genesis), disable, re-enable and adoption, strict mode, the
commit path, each changeset operation and the records it writes, statement identity, the immutable revision table,
history reads, stats, the Python changeset and client commit (including commits nested in a caller's transaction,
4.7.0), post-commit properties, the strict-mode guard and the error mapping. Production files:
iris_src/src/Graph/KG/Ledger.cls (except the Records/CanonicalRecord readers and the spec 256 redaction checks, owned
by the orchestrator's modules), LedgerApply.cls, LedgerGenesis.cls, LedgerStats.cls, LedgerRevision.cls (the
redaction exemption in RejectUpdate belongs to the redaction module), iris_vector_graph/ledger/changeset.py,
client.py (enable, disable, set_strict, meta, head, commit, history, stats; not redact, the record readers or
verify), guard.py, errors.py, __init__.py and iris_vector_graph/_engine/ledger.py.
