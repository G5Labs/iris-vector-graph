# 03 Direct writes

Module: WRT
Root: WRT-0
Scope: Direct (non-ledger) writes of nodes, edges, labels and properties, their write transactions, and bulk loads. Production files: iris_vector_graph/_engine/nodes_edges.py (all but erase_graph, delete_nodes_by_prefix and drop_graph, which the erasure module owns), iris_src/src/Graph/KG/PropWrite.cls, iris_src/src/Graph/KG/Txn.cls, iris_src/src/Graph/KG/LabelIndex.cls, iris_vector_graph/_write_txn.py, iris_vector_graph/bulk_loader.py, iris_vector_graph/prop_values.py, iris_vector_graph/_dictionary.py, iris_vector_graph/_collation.py, iris_vector_graph/_batch_check.py, and the write facade of iris_vector_graph/engine.py. Graph names, keys and modes are module GID; the ledger, the eraser and the adjacency writer are cited where a direct write calls them.
