# Decisions for the human: 18 Snapshots

### HD-1801 What should a restore do to the live ledger?
- Owner: human
- Question: a snapshot archive carries the whole `^IVG.Ledger` and the ledger tables' storage
  (`snapshot.py:399-404`). A replacing restore erases content but keeps the ledger's records (`Eraser.cls:806-812`),
  then writes the archive's ledger node by node on top (`snapshot.py:1554-1588`, `:1679`); a merge restore does the
  same without the erase. When the live ledger is not the archive's (it moved on, it was redacted since, or the
  archive has no ledger), the result mixes two histories (finding F-1801). Spec 213 FR-043 asks only that ledger
  storage "survives snapshot save/restore"; ADR-0013 puts backups "out of reach" of redaction but does not say what a
  restore of one may do.
- Options: (a) a restore replaces the ledger wholesale (kill `^IVG.Ledger` and the storage globals, then load), and
  says so; (b) a restore refuses when the ledger is enabled and its head is not the archive's head, unless the caller
  passes an explicit flag; (c) a restore never touches the ledger: it skips the ledger globals and leaves `verify()`
  to report the difference; (d) (b) plus re-applying the live redactions after a replace, so a restore cannot bring
  back redacted content.
- Default the code applies today: overlay; the ledger after a restore can hold records past its head, and an older
  archive puts redacted records back. The cells (namespace with the ledger on, replacing or merge restore) stay open
  until this is decided.

### HD-1802 Should a snapshot be a point-in-time view?
- Owner: human
- Question: `save_snapshot` reads each table, then each global, one after another, with no transaction, lock or
  ledger head check (`snapshot.py:864-1034`). Writes that land during a save can appear in one store and not another
  (a row without its vector, a ledger head ahead of the rows). The guides describe a snapshot as a copy "of every
  graph's rows and globals" without saying when.
- Options: (a) document that a snapshot is not a point-in-time view and should be taken while writers are stopped;
  (b) take the ledger head lock (or a namespace-wide write lock) for the duration and record the head in the
  metadata; (c) read everything inside one transaction at a snapshot isolation level IRIS offers, if any.
- Default the code applies today: no guarantee and no documentation of it.
