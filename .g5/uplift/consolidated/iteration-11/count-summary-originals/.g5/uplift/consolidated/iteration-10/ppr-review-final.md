## What is established

The model derives scoped node membership and distinct valid seeds from raw rows,
eligible forward/reverse slots from graph/predicate/count fields, and the divisor
from slot count. Scatter, dangling mass and seed restart produce each new score
vector. Initial/repeated exact mass1 follows for a closed stable graph in forward
or unit-reverse mode. Nonunit reverse scales the contribution, not the divisor;
the documented two-node mass3/4 and3/2 neighbors remain separate as-built behavior.

Actual new-score installation, L1 change including disappearing coordinates,
nonzero held count, strict tolerance comparison and finite iteration cap decide
stopping. Zero/negative tolerance is retained. This is finite exact recurrence and
termination, with no input matrix, supplied mass/rank map, normalized transition
table or converged flag. It proves no infinite convergence or general native
floating accuracy bound.

The public mathematical request preserves exclusionNone versus Some([]), producer
default1000, explicit caller top and final reader cap. Full fallback sorts all
node scores; fast completion ranks/caps scored rows and appends current absent
IDs at zero before cutting. Both counterexamples follow from the recurrence:

  edge and alpha1e-13. The alpha1e-11 neighbor agrees. q008 still leaves the desired
  zero-tie policy open; the package does not approve either policy or a repair.
- **104**'s generic theorem covers every distinct raw node list N>1000 with all nodes
  isolated valid seeds. It derives positive uniform initial/one-step scores and
