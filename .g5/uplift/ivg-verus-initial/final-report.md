## 1. What was done

1. **Stage A — intent.** Six domain workers recovered intent from ADRs, specs and their recorded decisions, CONTEXT.md, guides, CHANGELOG/KNOWN_ISSUES, history and code; a seventh built an independent operation inventory from code entry points (≈126 state-changing and 130 read-only entry points). The ontology was drafted per domain, then a fresh reader reconstructed the product from the ontology alone and compared it with the inventory and sources; its 25 corrections were verified against sources and applied (several rejected with reasons).
2. **Stage B — models and binding.** Five Verus models (328 obligations, 128 faulty controls, all `EXPECTED_FAIL`), one per domain, with L1 rules from the governing sources, L2 transitions at real commit/lock boundaries, and witnesses wherever the as-built mechanism violates L1. The core model has a Verus-verified executable twin (51 obligations) used by the binding probe to classify real ledger records. 35 native probes (40 observations) drove only public product entry points on this arm's IRIS (Community, container `ivg-g5-verus-20261113-iris`). The upstream unit suite, every ledger/erasure integration file and every e2e/integration file the ontology cites as coverage (≈110 files) were run against the baseline.
3. **Stage C — independent review.** Round 1: three fresh reviewers (core, data, platform) assessed coverage and fidelity, ran adversarial controls, and produced 38 material findings; all were adjudicated (accepted, modified or dismissed with reasons — [plan](uplift-plan.md#round-1-adjudication)) and repaired in the owning artifacts, then re-run on the final candidate. Round 2: two fresh reviewers (R2-core with cross-domain reach, R2-domains) re-assessed coverage and fidelity on the integrated artifacts, and R2-core reproduced the runbook from a byte-identical scratch mirror of `.g5/`; their 13 findings were all accepted ([plan](uplift-plan.md#round-2-adjudication)) — among them a prefix erase that misses temporal-only events ([bug-led-007](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-led-007), observed), a BM25 delete lost during a Build ([bug-srch-016](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-srch-016), observed), an overclaimed resume proof (downgraded to Observed) and a purge decide step that was not product-faithful. Round 3 (final, at the cap of three): R3-validation checked the round-2 repairs and made a last coverage/fidelity pass with seven adversarial model edits ([report](review/round3-validation.md); R3-1…R3-8, none Critical or High), and R3-reader read the ontology as a rebuilder against the pre-round-2 snapshot and the stage-A notes ([report](review/round3-reader.md); 4 High, 25 Medium, 11 Low). Round-3 repairs went to uplift artifacts only — a product-faithful property row, witness W6, an erase frame lemma and two controls in the core model; a native probe for the orphan-property case; coverage rows for every omitted core clause; register corrections and new entries; ontology body corrections ([dispositions](review/round3-reader-repairs.md)) — and were validated by re-running the proofs, all controls, the twin, the probe, both checkers and the freshness gate. No fourth review round was started.

## 3. What may be relied on

Details and every claim's row are in the [coverage map](../../Verus_verification/coverage.md). In short:

- **Proved** (Verus, all sizes, named premises): target matching and the separator boundary; tombstone-in-place and the frame for every other record; batch composition within one call's knowledge; completion of a started run (serialised redactions); the erase graph frame (another graph's content is untouched); commit admission order, replay-first and redacted-key refusal; replay equals the store after erase + redact (no unrecorded writes); graph-key injectivity and disjointness; the locked BM25 writers' counters; serial schema setup; strict-mode refusal on guarded paths; temporal first-write-wins and marked-row rules for the serial cases.
- **Observed** on this arm (finite): every proved core behaviour above on real records; resume of a paused graph or prefix redaction across calls; strict mode on guarded paths; BM25 cutoff contract; lock retention for BM25 inside a caller's transaction; one-schema refusal for ordinary engines; upstream suites (unit 24,792 passed; ledger/erasure integration all passed per file; 1,330 cited e2e/integration tests passed).
- **Violated / observed defects**: 28 register bugs are reproduced natively (most also by a model witness), including 11 of the 13 High entries; the other two High entries are suspected (source review and model only). Index: [register](../../Verus_verification/findings.md#high-severity-bugs).
- **Assumed** (not checked here): IRIS transactions undo SQL and journaled globals together (erasure's failure-injection suites could not run on Community); the head lock is exclusive and held to transaction end; `$Increment` survives rollback (its consequences were observed); CRC-32's suffix-forging property; the twin's line adapter.
- **Open**: everything under §6.

No statement is tagged `implementation-verified`: no product executable body was verified (the product is ObjectScript and Python). Guarantees are all **new** (no prior foundation existed); none was inherited.

## 4. Product findings (cross-linked)

Register totals: [findings.md](../../Verus_verification/findings.md). High-severity bugs:

- [bug-001](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-001) (ledger/redaction, observed) — Two redactions of different targets can run at once, and each one's audit and receipt then describes the other's work.
- [bug-002](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-002) (ledger/erasure, observed) — A redaction completes while its target's content is still uncommitted-deleted, and the caller's rollback brings the content back with its history tombstoned.
- [bug-str-001](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-str-001) (graph structure, observed) — Deleting one of several parallel edges through the ledger removes the adjacency of the edges that remain.
- [bug-str-002](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-str-002) (graph structure, observed) — A cached algorithm answer computed over a write that later rolls back stays current.
- [bug-tmp-001](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-tmp-001) (temporal, observed) — A temporal write whose structural step fails keeps its raw entry, cannot be finished by a retry, and the bulk fallback reports it written.
- [bug-tmp-002](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-tmp-002) (temporal, observed) — An update at an occupied temporal coordinate counts the edge twice in the statistics.
- [bug-tmp-003](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-tmp-003) (temporal, observed) — A rolled-back temporal write leaves its statistics counted, so a retry counts the edge twice.
- [bug-tmp-010](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-tmp-010) (temporal, observed) — A rolled-back temporal delete or node erase leaves the bucket's event counter decremented.
- [bug-srch-001](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-srch-001) (search, observed) — Search indexes keep answering with content that was erased, and a re-created node inherits the erased text.
- [bug-srch-002](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-srch-002) (search, observed) — A failed BM25 insert or delete rolls back the caller's whole transaction.
- [bug-srch-007](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-srch-007) (search, observed) — Claiming the default embedding table for a model that already has a default-graph route makes that model's stored vectors unreadable.
- [bug-plt-001](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-plt-001) (install, suspected) — The 4.0.0 documents migration can drop the documents table after its copy failed with a tolerated error.
- [bug-cyp-001](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-cyp-001) (Cypher, suspected) — A caller with Cypher read access can forge a CRC-32 collision so that another shape's queries run its compiled code.

bug-led-007 (prefix erase leaves temporal-only events) was High until round 3 and is now Medium: spec 256 deferred tenant-scoped temporal erase inside a shared graph, and ADR-0013's recipe assumes one graph per tenant; it stays Medium because the redaction reports `complete` without saying anything is left.

Added in review round 3: [bug-led-010](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-led-010) (an orphan property row blocks prefix redaction; observed), and, suspected from code reading, [bug-led-011](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-led-011) (per-500 commits in long erases), [bug-plt-026](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-plt-026) (an explicit schema recorded against FR-009), [bug-plt-027](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-plt-027) (BulkLoader returns before rebuilding), [bug-srch-017](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-srch-017) (a pre-4.0.0 BM25 index read as the default graph's) and [bug-str-017](../../Verus_verification/bugs_issues.md#bug-str-017) (refused graph names are never reported); questions [q-plt-018](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-plt-018), [q-plt-019](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-plt-019) and [q-srch-006](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-srch-006). bug-str-016 moved from the observations to the suspected bugs.

Selected simplifications (each with the behaviour it must preserve): [simp-001](../../Verus_verification/design_simplification.md#simp-001) claim the redaction marker for one run under the head lock (removes bug-001's race); [simp-002](../../Verus_verification/design_simplification.md#simp-002) carry endpoints in every relationship record (removes bug-004); [simp-003](../../Verus_verification/design_simplification.md#simp-003) derive redaction's liveness check from the eraser's store inventory (removes bug-003 and the temporal gap; depends on first repairing the prefix erase, bug-led-007 and bug-led-010). All: [design_simplification.md](../../Verus_verification/design_simplification.md).

Decisions needed from a human (High/Medium): [findings.md § decisions](../../Verus_verification/findings.md#decisions-needed-high-and-medium-questions). The most consequential: serialisation of erase, redact and commit ([q-004](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-004)); whether temporal-only content counts as live ([q-009](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-009)); the strict-mode erase-then-redact path ([q-003](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-003)); another tenant's retry of a shared revision ([q-001](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-001)); post-commit properties vs verify ([q-010](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-010)); prefix rules for erase vs redact ([q-008](../../Verus_verification/open_questions.md#q-008)).
