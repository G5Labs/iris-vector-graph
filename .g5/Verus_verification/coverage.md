# Coverage map — governing clause → model → product → evidence

One map for the whole foundation. Each row answers the four handoff questions: what is required, what has formal support, what connects it to the product, and what another change may rely on. Product baseline `3eaa79cf`; final candidate receipts are under [`results/`](results), native observations under [`receipts/native/`](receipts/native) (hash-stamped, [sources](receipts/native/SOURCES.txt)), raw logs in `$RUN_DIR`.

Status words, used strictly:

- **Proved**: a Verus theorem over the named model, for all sizes, under the listed premises. A proof is about the model; it says nothing about the product without a binding.
- **Observed**: the unmodified product, driven through its public operations on this arm's IRIS (Community, Python 3.11.18, driver 5.3.2), behaved as stated (native probe or upstream live test). Finite: these inputs, this configuration.
- **Violated**: a model witness and a native observation both show the governing rule failing; the finding is linked.
- **Suspected violation**: a model witness (or source reading) shows a failing trace; no native reproduction.
- **Assumed**: a premise nobody checked here (an IRIS guarantee, a trusted adapter, an abstraction).
- **Open**: not established; next action named.

"May rely on": *supported within scope*, *conditional on <premise>*, *confirmed violation (finding)*, *unresolved authority (question)*, or *unestablished*. No statement is `implementation-verified`: no product executable body was verified.
